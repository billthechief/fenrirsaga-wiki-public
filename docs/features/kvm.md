# 🗡️ Custom KVM Battlegrounds

A deathmatch-style Battlegrounds mode.

* Team size: **3 players**.
* Players earn reward points during a match.
* Point turn-in is currently disabled. An NPC to exchange points for headgear rewards has been announced.

### 🎁 Entry Freebies
Everyone entering the arena receives:

* 150 Slim White Potions
* 20 Blue Potions
* 10 Green Potions

Class-specific additions:

* **Alchemist / Chemist:** 20 Acid Bottles, 20 Fire Bottles
* **Assassin Cross:** 5 Poison Bottles
* **Priest / High Priest:** 20 Blue Gems, 3 Holy Water
* **Wizard / High Wizard:** 20 Blue Gems, 10 Red Gems
* **Sage / Scholar:** 20 Blue Gems, 20 Yellow Gems, 10 Red Gems
* **Hunter / Sniper:** 20 Traps, 300 Arrows
* **Monk / Champion:** 30 extra Blue Potions

Free items stack with your existing inventory and are consumed first. They're removed when you leave the arena or log out.
